# Traffic Safety Dashboard | Excel Data Analysis

 📂 Download Traffic Safety Dashboard Excel Dashboard 
 https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BW4yJGKpsRCUg1SX_1uDExdUar0kYuVw/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=102827734465052016393&rtpof=true&sd=true

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Traffic Safety Dashboard using Microsoft Excel** to analyze road accidents, casualties, accident causes, vehicle types, geographical distribution, and seasonal patterns. The dashboard consolidates key performance indicators (KPIs) and multiple visualizations to identify accident trends, highlight major contributing factors, and support data-driven traffic safety decisions.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Accidents Reported:** 238.4K
- **Total People Injured:** 116.2K
- **Total Fatalities:** 635
- **Injury Rate:** 48.75%
- **Fatality Rate:** 0.27%
- **Average Injuries per Accident:** 0.49

## Visualizations and Analysis

### 1. Accidents by Time of Day – Column Chart

Analyzes accident frequency across six time intervals:

- 12 AM–4 AM
- 12 PM–4 PM
- 4 AM–8 AM
- 4 PM–8 PM
- 8 AM–12 PM
- 8 PM–12 AM

The visualization highlights differences in accident frequency throughout the day, with the 12 PM–4 PM interval recording the highest count at approximately 52.5K accidents.

### 2. Top 10 Accident Causes – Horizontal Bar Chart

Ranks the leading reported causes of road accidents, including:

- Unspecified
- Driver Inattention/Distraction
- Failure to Yield Right-of-Way
- Following Too Closely
- Passing or Lane Usage Improper
- Passing Too Closely
- Unsafe Speed
- Backing Unsafely
- Traffic Control Disregarded
- Other Vehicular Factors

Driver inattention/distraction and right-of-way violations stand out among the leading specified causes, highlighting the importance of driver awareness and compliance with traffic rules.

### 3. Monthly Accident Trend – Line Chart

Tracks accident counts from January through December to identify monthly fluctuations and periods of increased accident frequency. March shows the highest point in the displayed trend, while accident counts generally decline toward the end of the year.

### 4. Accidents by Borough – Doughnut Chart

Compares accident distribution across New York City boroughs:

- Brooklyn
- Queens
- Manhattan
- Bronx
- Staten Island
- Unknown/Other

Brooklyn contributes the largest share at approximately 30%, followed by Queens at around 27%. This comparison helps identify geographical areas that may require additional traffic-safety attention.

### 5. Vehicle Type vs. Accident Count – Line Chart

Compares accident counts across vehicle categories, including passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, motorcycles, bicycles, and other vehicle types. The chart contains a prominent spike for one category, suggesting a need to verify category labels and the underlying aggregation before drawing conclusions about vehicle-specific risk.

### 6. Seasonal Accident Trend – Horizontal Bar Chart

Compares accidents across the four seasons:

- **Spring:** 64,021
- **Winter:** 61,179
- **Summer:** 57,752
- **Autumn:** 55,462

Spring records the highest accident count, followed by winter, summer, and autumn. The results highlight seasonal variation in accident frequency.

## Key Insights

- **High accident volume:** Approximately 238.4K accidents were reported in the dataset, emphasizing the importance of effective road-safety measures.
- **Injury burden:** The dashboard reports 116.2K injured people, with an injury rate of 48.75%.
- **Driver behaviour matters:** Inattention/distraction and right-of-way violations are among the leading specified accident causes.
- **Afternoon accident peak:** The 12 PM–4 PM interval records the highest accident count among the displayed time periods.
- **Geographical concentration:** Brooklyn and Queens account for the largest displayed borough shares, indicating areas worth examining for targeted safety interventions.
- **Seasonal variation:** Spring records the highest seasonal accident count, while autumn has the lowest.
- **Monthly fluctuations:** Accident counts vary across months, demonstrating the value of ongoing trend monitoring.
- **Fatalities and injuries:** The dashboard reports 635 fatalities and an average of 0.49 injuries per accident. These metrics should be interpreted in the context of the dataset's definitions and reporting coverage.

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes slicers and filters for:

- Month
- Season
- Cross Street
- Contributing Factor

These interactive controls allow users to explore accident patterns across different time periods, locations, and reported contributing factors.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Interactive Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Data Visualization
- Trend and Comparative Analysis
- Traffic Safety Data Analysis

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard in Microsoft Excel to evaluate revenue, order volume, pizza category performance, customer ordering patterns, and product preferences. The dashboard combines KPI cards, charts, and interactive slicers to identify top-performing products and sales trends.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** $2.95M
- **Average Order Value:** $17.07
- **Total Pizzas Sold:** 178.9K
- **Total Orders:** 172.8K
- **Top Revenue Category:** Classic – $787.65K
- **Peak Sales Month:** November – $262.29K

## Visualizations and Analysis

1. **Sales by Pizza Category – Column Chart**  
   Compares revenue across Chicken ($703.7K), Classic ($787.6K), Supreme ($758.7K), and Veggie ($701.1K). Classic generates the highest revenue among the displayed categories.

2. **Top Performer – Most-Ordered Pizza**  
   Highlights The Thai Chicken Pizza as the most-ordered pizza in the dashboard.

3. **Orders by Day of the Week – Horizontal Bar Chart**  
   Compares order volumes across Monday to Sunday, helping identify stronger and weaker ordering days.

4. **Orders by Pizza Size – Doughnut Chart**  
   Shows the distribution of orders across Large, Medium, Small, X-Large, and XX-Large pizzas, allowing comparison of customer size preferences.

5. **Top 10 Loved Pizzas – Horizontal Bar Chart**  
   Ranks ten pizza varieties by order volume, including The Thai Chicken Pizza, The Spicy Italian Pizza, The Southwest Chicken Pizza, The Sicilian Pizza, and The Pepperoni Pizza.

6. **Preferred Size – Highlight Card**  
   Identifies Large as the most-ordered pizza size.

7. **Interactive Slicers and Filters**  
   Includes filters for month, pizza size, category, pizza name, and quantity to explore sales performance across different product and time-period selections.

## Key Insights
- Total revenue reaches $2.95M, with 178.9K pizzas sold and 172.8K orders recorded.
- The Classic category leads category revenue at approximately $787.65K.
- November is the peak sales month, generating $262.29K.
- The Thai Chicken Pizza is highlighted as the most-ordered pizza.
- Large pizzas are identified as the preferred size.
- Category revenue is relatively close across Chicken, Classic, Supreme, and Veggie, although Classic leads the group.
- Day-of-week and top-pizza charts provide opportunities to examine ordering patterns and product demand.

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive dashboard to explore the relationship between social media usage, sleep duration, academic performance, mental health indicators, and relationship conflicts among students. The dashboard transforms student-related data into visual insights to better understand the potential impact of digital habits on student well-being.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Average Screen Time:** 4.92 hours
- **Zombie Score:** 6.87
- **Red Flag Level:** 6.44
- **Drama Index:** 2.85
- **Grade Drop Rate:** 64.26%

## Visualizations and Analysis

1. **Top 5 Most-Used Social Media Platforms – Horizontal Bar Chart**  
   Compares platform usage across Instagram (249), TikTok (154), Facebook (123), WhatsApp (54), and Twitter (30). Instagram leads the displayed figures.

2. **Top 5 Countries by Average Daily Social Media Usage – Horizontal Bar Chart**  
   Compares average daily usage across the USA, UAE, Mexico, Ecuador, and India. The USA records the highest average among the displayed countries.

3. **Impact of Social Media Usage on Sleep Hours – Line Chart**  
   Visualizes the relationship between average social media usage and average sleep hours. The displayed trend shows sleep duration declining as social media usage increases.

4. **Average Social Media Conflict by Relationship Status – Bar Chart**  
   Compares conflict scores for complicated relationships (3.03), single students (2.90), and students in relationships (2.76). The complicated category records the highest average score.

5. **Average Daily Social Media Usage by Education Level – Column Chart**  
   Compares average usage among graduates (4.78), high school students (5.54), and undergraduates (5.00). High school students record the highest average usage.

6. **Average Mental Health Score by Digital Addiction Score – Line Chart**  
   Shows a downward trend in the displayed mental health score as digital addiction scores increase, highlighting an association worth investigating further.

7. **Interactive Filters**  
   Includes filters for age, gender, and academic level to explore patterns across different student groups.

## Key Insights
- Instagram is the most-used platform among the five displayed platforms.
- High school students have the highest average daily social media usage among the education groups shown.
- The displayed sleep trend suggests that higher social media usage is associated with fewer hours of sleep.
- The complicated relationship-status group records the highest average social media conflict score.
- The mental health score decreases as the digital addiction score rises in the displayed data.
- The grade drop rate KPI stands at 64.26%, highlighting an academic performance indicator worth further analysis.
